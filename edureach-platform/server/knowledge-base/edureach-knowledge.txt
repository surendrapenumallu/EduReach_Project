Scholarships & Financial Assistance:
- Merit Scholarship: Up to 100% tuition fee waiver for students ranking in top 5,000 TS EAMCET or top 10,000 JEE Main.
- Means Scholarship: 50% tuition fee concession for families with annual income below ₹2.5 Lakhs.
- Women Empowerment Grant: 25% tuition fee waiver for all female engineering candidates.
- Sports & Defense Quota: Special fee waivers for state/national sports achievers and children of armed forces personnel.

Admissions Timeline & Eligibility Criteria:
- Admissions open annually on March 1st.
- B.Tech Eligibility: Passed 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics (PCM) with minimum 60% aggregate. Entrance score accepted: TS EAMCET or JEE Main.
- Direct / Management Quota: 30% seats reserved for direct admission based on 10+2 PCM merit (minimum 75% required).
- NRI / Foreign Student Desk: Dedicated desk assisting international applicants with visa and lateral entry admissions.
